# Product Case Studies

A collection of product case studies exploring customer journeys, product opportunities, trade-offs and decision-making through structured analysis, prototyping and systems thinking.

The cases combine outside-in product exploration with generalized or anonymized product scenarios. They are created independently for learning, portfolio and product-thinking purposes.

## Cases

### Marketplace Bidding Experience

An outside-in exploration of a B2B marketplace experience, focusing on bidder / buyer confidence, seller transparency, marketplace trust and selected product opportunities.

The case includes:

- marketplace and journey mapping
- observed patterns and open questions
- bidder / buyer and seller opportunity areas
- lifecycle communication ideas
- clickable prototypes for Saved Search Management and Seller Ratings & Transparency

[Open the case](https://annika-mann.github.io/product-case-studies/cases/marketplace-bidding-experience/)

### FinTech Portfolio Reporting

A product exploration of portfolio reporting within a digital financial advisory workflow — moving from an isolated reporting output towards a connected part of analysis, scenario exploration, reusable client artifacts and ongoing communication.

The case includes:

- FinTech platform, advisor and workflow context
- success metrics, product levers and prioritised UX / workflow opportunities
- robustness and trust considerations for reporting automation
- interactive concepts for a client-ready Reporting Workflow, Document Management and Collaborative Scenarios
- product evolution through usage signals and a Now / Next / Future framing
- bilingual DE / EN presentation

[Open the case](https://annika-mann.github.io/product-case-studies/cases/fintech-portfolio-reporting/)

### Cloud Platform Stability

A systems-thinking product case about sequencing feature delivery when a shared cloud platform dependency is already live, but not yet reliable enough to support another customer-facing commitment.

The case includes:

- framing two legitimate customer needs around one shared platform dependency
- an explicit trade-off between feature delivery and platform stability
- an Investigate → Stabilise → Extend sequence with decision gates
- stakeholder-specific communication for customers, product and engineering
- readiness criteria, reliability-budget thinking and platform-health considerations
- reflections on making dependency and sequencing decisions visible earlier

[Open the case](https://annika-mann.github.io/product-case-studies/cases/cloud-platform-stability/)

## About this repository

The work in this repository is intended to show how I approach product problems: understanding the wider system, separating evidence from assumptions, identifying opportunity spaces, making trade-offs explicit and turning ideas into tangible product concepts or decision frameworks.

Where relevant, company-specific information has been removed or generalized. The cases do not represent internal company information or commissioned work.
