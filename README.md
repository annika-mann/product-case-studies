# Product Case Studies

A collection of outside-in product case studies exploring customer journeys, product opportunities and decision-making through structured analysis, prototyping and systems thinking.

These cases are based on publicly observable product experiences and are created independently for learning, portfolio and product-thinking purposes.

## Cases

### Marketplace Bidding Experience

An outside-in exploration of a B2B marketplace experience, focusing on bidder / buyer confidence, seller transparency, marketplace trust and selected product opportunities.

The case includes:

- marketplace and journey mapping
- observed patterns and open questions
- bidder / buyer and seller opportunity areas
- lifecycle communication ideas
- clickable prototypes for Saved Search Management and Seller Ratings & Transparency

[Open the case](./cases/marketplace-bidding-experience/)

## About this repository

The work in this repository is intended to show how I approach product problems from the outside in: understanding the wider system, separating evidence from assumptions, identifying opportunity spaces and making ideas tangible through prototypes.

Where relevant, company-specific information has been removed or generalized. The cases do not represent internal company information or commissioned work.
